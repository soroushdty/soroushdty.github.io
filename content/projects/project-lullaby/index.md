A digital health surveillance system for low-income mothers with pregnancy-induced hypertension (PIH)
in South Phoenix and Mesa, Arizona.

<!--more-->

The system combines passive monitoring with heat-risk context and timely clinical escalation, targeting
stroke and maternal mortality prevention in a population where both heat exposure and access barriers
compound cardiovascular risk.

Written primarily in Python and released under GPL-3.0.
