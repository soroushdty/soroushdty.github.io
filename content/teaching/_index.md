## Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy bridges **clinical medicine**, **computational methods**, and **data literacy**. Having trained as a physician (M.D.) and working in Biomedical Informatics (PhD), I focus on helping students understand both the technical mechanics of health data systems and their real-world impact on clinical workflows and patient care.

---

## Courses Supported

### BMI 201: Introduction to Clinical Informatics
**Role:** Graduate Teaching Associate  
**Institution:** College of Health Solutions, Arizona State University  
**Term:** Fall 2025  

* **Overview:** Foundational course covering clinical data structures, electronic health record (EHR) architectures, clinical decision support (CDS) systems, and health data interoperability.
* **Responsibilities:**
  - Led weekly discussion sections and student office hours.
  - Developed and graded practical assignments on clinical data standards (ICD-10, CPT, LOINC, SNOMED-CT).
  - Guided undergraduate students in building introductory clinical data queries.

### BMI 601: Health Informatics
**Role:** Graduate Teaching Associate  
**Institution:** College of Health Solutions, Arizona State University  
**Term:** Spring 2026  

* **Overview:** Graduate-level course focusing on advanced health informatics methodologies, FHIR-based interoperability, clinical natural language processing (NLP), and AI evaluation in healthcare.
* **Responsibilities:**
  - Facilitated graduate-level case study discussions on clinical AI safety and algorithmic bias.
  - Assisted in designing hands-on labs using Python and FHIR APIs.
  - Mentored master's and doctoral students on term research projects in clinical data analysis.

## Mentorship & Student Guidance

- **Office Hours & Project Coaching:** Dedicated weekly sessions supporting students in debugging Python pipelines, understanding clinical workflows, and designing informatics research projects.
- **Academic Writing Workshops:** Organized peer-review and scientific writing sessions to help students format abstracts and manuscripts for health informatics conferences.
