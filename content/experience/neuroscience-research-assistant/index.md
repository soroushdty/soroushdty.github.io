## Overview

**Role:** Research Assistant  
**Organization:** Neuroscience Research Center, Department of Physiology, IAUTMU, Tehran, Iran  
**Principal Collaborator & Advisor:** Dr. Batool Ghorbani Yekta  
**Period:** May 2018 – January 2020  

---

## 🔬 Research Focus & Technical Contributions

During my appointment at the Neuroscience Research Center under the mentorship of **Dr. Batool Ghorbani Yekta**, I led data analysis, experimental design, and statistical visualization across multiple physiological and neuropharmacological research projects:

- **Experimental Animal Models & Neuropharmacology:** Managed longitudinal data acquisition, curve fitting, and group comparison statistics for experimental rodent models evaluating intrauterine drug exposure.
- **Biostatistical Modeling & Visualization:** Performed ANOVA, regression modeling, and figure generation for peer-reviewed journal submissions.
- **Interdisciplinary Clinical Translation:** Applied physiological modeling to clinical diagnostic studies in reproductive endocrinology, vascular surgery, and renal function.
