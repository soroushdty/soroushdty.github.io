## Position Overview

**Role:** Family Physician (General Practitioner)  
**Organization:** Qeshlaq Rural Health Center, Abyek, Qazvin, Iran  
**Period:** October 2024 – July 2025  
**Population Served:** 7,500+ residents across 17 rural villages and 5 satellite health centers  
**Licensure:** Registered Physician, Medical Council of Iran (IRIMC)  

---

## 🩺 Clinical Responsibilities & Panel Management

As the primary physician for a rural population of over 7,500 individuals, I provided comprehensive primary, preventive, emergency, and chronic disease care:

- **Comprehensive Outpatient Triage & Care:** Managed high-volume daily outpatient clinics, diagnosing and treating acute illnesses, infectious diseases, pediatric conditions, and geriatric syndromes.
- **Chronic Disease Registries:** Managed active clinical registries for hypertension, Type 2 diabetes, ischemic heart disease, and asthma, adjusting pharmacotherapy and lifestyle protocols to achieve target clinical benchmarks.
- **Maternal, Prenatal & Adolescent Health:** Conducted routine prenatal screenings, high-risk pregnancy monitoring, growth and development tracking, and pediatric immunization schedules.
- **Mental & Behavioral Health Integration:** Provided primary psychiatric evaluation and pharmacological management for depression, anxiety, and stress disorders in underserved rural communities.

---

## 💻 Health Informatics & EHR Operations in Frontline Practice

- **National EHR Operations:** Utilized the Iranian national Electronic Health Record system (**SIB**) for coding diagnoses (ICD-10), managing e-prescriptions, ordering laboratory investigations, and coordinating specialist referrals.
- **Real-World Health Data Insights:** Navigated the operational challenges of EHR data entry, coding ambiguity, and clinical workflow friction in high-throughput settings. This frontline experience directly drives my current PhD research on **trustworthy clinical AI, FHIR data segmentation, and evidence-grounded clinical decision support**.

---

## 🚑 Emergency Response & Escalation Pathways

- **Acute Medical Emergency Management:** Served as first responder for acute trauma, cardiac arrests, acute coronary syndromes, severe asthma exacerbations, and pediatric emergencies in rural satellite posts.
- **Clinical Escalation Protocols:** Established rapid stabilization protocols and coordinated emergency ambulance transport to tertiary referral hospitals in Qazvin and Tehran.
